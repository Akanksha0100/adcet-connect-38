Welcome to the ADCET Alumni Network
The ADCET Alumni Network is a vibrant and ever-growing community of achievers who continue to uphold the values and legacy of Annasaheb Dange College of Engineering and Technology (ADCET), Ashta. Our alumni have established themselves as successful professionals, entrepreneurs, researchers, academicians, innovators, administrators, and leaders across India and around the world. Through their achievements, they exemplify the excellence, integrity, and commitment to society nurtured during their time at ADCET.
Since the inception of the institute, thousands of ADCET graduates have made significant contributions across diverse sectors including engineering, information technology, manufacturing, infrastructure, research, higher education, public service, and entrepreneurship. Their accomplishments reflect the quality of education, practical learning, and value-based culture that define ADCET.
The ADCET Alumni Network serves as a lifelong bridge between the institute and its graduates. It provides a platform to reconnect with classmates, engage with faculty, mentor current students, contribute to institutional development, share professional expertise, and foster meaningful collaborations. Through guest lectures, internships, placements, industry interactions, scholarships, and alumni chapters, our graduates continue to play a vital role in shaping the future of the institute and empowering the next generation of engineers.
Every alumnus is an integral part of the ADCET family. Whether you graduated recently or many years ago, your journey, achievements, and experiences inspire our students and strengthen our institution. Together, let us continue to build a strong, connected, and impactful alumni community that celebrates our shared heritage while creating new opportunities for future generations.
